1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL OVERVIEW
================================================================================
SHGConnect is an offline-first operating system and cryptographic record-keeping 
system designed for Self-Help Groups (SHGs) across rural India. It digitizes physical 
meeting registers, attendance tracking, monthly pool savings, internal loan 
disbursals, EMI repayments, cash box reconciliation, and NABARD Panchasutra audits 
without requiring blockchain gas fees, third-party cloud dependence, or constant internet.

The system combines:
1. Client-side append-only SHA-256 cryptographic hash chains with Merkle block 
   checkpoints (CHK-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX).
2. Local-first IndexedDB v3 transactional persistence with Optimistic Concurrency 
   Control (OCC entityVersion).
3. Multi-tenant offline synchronization engine with an idempotent outbox queue 
   (opId), monotonic sequence cursors, and conflict detection.
4. Persistent Node.js/PostgreSQL transactional synchronization backend with row-level 
   locking (SELECT ... FOR UPDATE) and outbox streaming.
5. 4-Tier Role-Based Operational Access Control (MEMBER, OFFICE_BEARER, ANIMATOR, AUDITOR).
6. Tri-lingual vernacular interface (Marathi, Hindi, English) with Web Speech API 
   voice synthesis and high-contrast rural touch ergonomics.

2. 4-TIER ROLE-BASED ACCESS ARCHITECTURE (RBAC)
================================================================================
The user interface and permissions strictly adapt across 4 distinct operational roles:

[Tier 1: MEMBER] - Read-Only Personal Finance & Group Health
- Dedicated Member Dashboard (MemberHome, SavingsView, LoansView, MeetingsView, MoreMenuView).
- Personalized greeting ("नमस्कार, कमलाताई 👋"), total savings display, active loan balances,
  and next meeting indicators.
- Personal passbook table, savings goals tracker, and loan EMI calculators.
- Cryptographic receipt viewing and UPI QR payment intent simulation.
- Read-only protection: Members cannot commit meetings or modify group ledger records.

[Tier 2: OFFICE_BEARER] (President, Secretary, Treasurer) - Operational Governance
- Dedicated OfficeBearerDashboard managing full SHG administrative operations.
- SHG Member Directory with individual trust scores (0-100) and member dossier inspections.
- Active group loans register with principal, remaining balance, and disbursal dates.
- Interactive 4-step Guided Meeting Wizard with 2-of-3 Officer PIN Quorum authentication.
- Cash Box Reconciliation module for counting physical Indian rupee notes and coins.
- Proceedings & Resolution Register for logging group motions and unanimous votes.
- Local cryptographic tamper attack simulation to demonstrate live chain verification.

[Tier 3: ANIMATOR / CRP (Prerak)] - Cluster / Village Portfolio Oversight
- Dedicated AnimatorDashboard supervising multi-SHG portfolios in the village.
- Village portfolio cards summarizing total groups, active beneficiaries, and Grade C alerts.
- Group-level health tracking (Grade A, B, C) and offline synchronization state monitoring.
- One-click trigger to force-sync outbox operations across all supervised groups.

[Tier 4: AUDITOR / BANK LINKAGE OFFICER] - Independent Forensic Compliance
- Dedicated AuditorDashboard designed for NABARD inspectors and bank linkage officers.
- Real-time SHA-256 ledger integrity forensics (validating hash linkages and block heights).
- Full NABARD Panchasutra operational audit card (evaluating 5 institutional pillars).
- Credit-readiness appraisal calculations with corpus multipliers and legal disclaimers.

3. CORE FUNCTIONAL MODULES IMPLEMENTED
================================================================================
3.1 Cryptographic Hash Chain & Merkle Checkpoints (hashChain.ts)
- Append-only block ledger: each transaction computes a canonical SHA-256 digest linking 
  the index, previous hash, timestamp, member ID, transaction type, amount, and notes.
- Merkle root aggregation for meeting transactions ensuring block-level integrity.
- Checkpoint fingerprints: truncated 16-character alphanumeric proofs (CHK-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) 
  generated from SHA-256 digests for human-readable physical receipt verification.
- Live tamper detection engine (verifyLedgerIntegrity) that recalculates hashes from 
  the genesis block and flags corrupted blocks.

3.2 Guided Meeting Wizard (MeetingWizard.tsx)
- Step 1 (Attendance): Interactive attendance roll call with attendance counts.
- Step 2 (Savings Collection & Cash Count): Per-member monthly quota entry, bulk 
  presets, audio transaction announcements via TTS, and total pool tallying.
- Step 3 (Resolutions & Loan Disbursal): Inline resolution entry and internal micro-loan 
  disbursal with purpose categorization.
- Step 4 (2-of-3 Multi-Signatory PIN Quorum): Multi-officer consensus requiring 2 
  valid 4-digit PINs from President, Secretary, or Treasurer verified against SHA-256 
  pinHash credentials stored in IndexedDB.

3.3 Cash Box Reconciliation (CashBoxReconciliation.tsx)
- Physical Indian currency denomination counter:
  ₹500, ₹200, ₹100, ₹50, ₹20, ₹10 notes and coins.
- Real-time calculation of physical cash vs. expected digital ledger collections.
- Discrepancy flagging (exact match / shortage / excess) prior to meeting block finalization.

3.4 NABARD Panchasutra Visualizer (PanchasutraVisualizer.tsx)
- Computes operational scores across all 5 core NABARD/NRLM principles (0-20 each, total 100):
  1. Regular Meetings (नियमित बैठक)
  2. Regular Savings (नियमित बचत)
  3. Internal Lending (अंतर्गत कर्जवाटप)
  4. Timely Recovery (वेळेवर परतफेड)
  5. Transparent Bookkeeping (पारदर्शक हिशोब)
- Institutional Grade Classification: Grade A (>=80), Grade B (>=60), Grade C (<60).
- Estimated internal credit readiness calculation and formal institutional disclaimer.

3.5 Offline Synchronization Engine (syncCoordinator.ts, syncApi.ts, mockSyncServer.ts)
- Transactional outbox queue (`outbox_queue`) storing atomic mutations with idempotency 
  keys (`opId`), Hybrid Logical Clock timestamps (`hlcTimestamp`), and actor context.
- Monotonic cursor tracking (`lastServerSeq`) per SHG group in `sync_metadata`.
- HTTP push/pull transport interacting with the remote PostgreSQL backend.
- Protected entity conflict detection routing divergent remote operations to `deferred_remote_ops`.
- Interactive Conflict Resolution Modal (ConflictResolutionModal.tsx) supporting 
  KEEP_LOCAL, ACCEPT_REMOTE, and RETRY_MERGED resolution strategies.
- Real-time status pill in header (SyncStatusPill.tsx) reflecting SYNCED, PENDING, 
  SYNCING, CONFLICT, or OFFLINE states.

3.6 AES-256-GCM Encrypted Backup & Restore (cryptoBackup.ts)
- Web Crypto API implementation for zero-cloud data portability.
- PBKDF2 key derivation (100,000 iterations, SHA-256) using user passphrase.
- AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption with 12-byte initialization vectors (IV).
- Generates password-protected `.shgbk` backup files containing all IndexedDB stores.
- Atomic restore engine with rollback validation.

3.7 Multi-Modal Accessibility & Vernacular i18n
- Trilingual localization in `src/i18n/translations.ts` supporting Marathi (mr), 
  Hindi (hi), and English (en).
- Web Speech API integration in `src/services/tts.ts` vocalizing financial deposits, 
  repayments, and announcements in regional languages.
- Web Audio API synthesizer in `src/services/sound.ts` producing stamp and chime feedback.
- Accessible UI tokens in `tokens.ts` and `tailwind.config.js` tailored for rural 
  outdoor contrast and minimum 48px touch bounding targets.
- Responsive application shell with desktop Sidebar and mobile bottom navigation (MobileNav).

4. PERSISTENT POSTGRESQL BACKEND ARCHITECTURE (server/)
================================================================================
The Node.js backend under `server/` provides persistent enterprise synchronization:
- Express 4 application with strict CORS, JSON parsing, and centralized error handling.
- `schema.sql`: PostgreSQL DDL defining sequences, idempotency tables, outbox logs, 
  and domain projections (`domain_members`, `domain_transactions`, `domain_loans`, `domain_meetings`).
- Atomic transactions (`BEGIN ... COMMIT`) with row-level locking (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`).
- Push endpoint (`POST /sync/push`): Validates idempotency keys (`processed_operations`), 
  enforces entityVersion OCC checks, assigns global monotonically increasing `server_seq`, 
  and projects domain state.
- Pull endpoint (`POST /sync/pull`): Streams serialized operation logs from `server_operation_log` 
  filtered by `shg_id` starting from `lastServerSeq`.
- Automated Integration Test Suite (`sync.integration.test.ts`): 11 tests verifying all 
  sync invariants against an in-memory PostgreSQL engine (`pg-mem`).

8. LIVE MULTI-DEVICE MEETING ROOM & DYNAMIC HANDSHAKE OTP PROTOCOL
================================================================================
1. Architecture & Real-Time Coordination:
   - In-memory meeting room coordinator via meetingRoomPlugin in vite.config.ts.
   - High-performance Server-Sent Events (SSE) push streaming over /api/meeting/:code/stream.
   - Endpoints:
     * POST /api/meeting/start               : Creates room with 4-digit code (e.g. 9757).
     * POST /api/meeting/:code/join          : Member enters code, triggers member_joined SSE event.
     * POST /api/meeting/:code/savings/initiate : Generates dynamic 4-digit OTP for specific member.
     * POST /api/meeting/:code/savings/verify   : Leader inputs member's OTP, commits verified savings.
     * POST /api/meeting/:code/savings/cancel   : Member rejects incorrect amount.
     * POST /api/meeting/:code/complete        : Finalizes meeting and issues receipts.

2. Physical Cash Handover Safeguard (Mutual Handshake OTP):
   - Solves the rural microfinance vulnerability: software cannot roll back physical notes.
   - When Leader enters savings (e.g. ₹500) and hits Save, a dynamic OTP (e.g. 7774) is pushed
     to the Member's screen.
   - Member inspects the exact amount on their phone and reads out the Handshake OTP.
   - Leader inputs the OTP on their device to commit the cash into the group pool.
   - Both devices trigger auditory confirmation and visual verified badges (पडताळले ✅).

3. Dashboard Integrations:
   - Leader: OfficeBearerDashboard action button '📡 थेट बैठक सुरू करा' opens LeadLiveMeeting.
   - Member: MemberDashboard Home banner + Meetings tab card '📡 थेट बैठक कक्ष' opens MemberLiveMeeting.
